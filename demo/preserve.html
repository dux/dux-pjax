Preservation across swaps

What survives a PJAX swap, and what doesn't.

Outside #pjax: persistent

The header, the menu, and the event log on the right live outside the morph target. They keep all internal state across navigations.

Click around the menu — the event log on the right keeps growing without resetting.

Inside #pjax: replaced

This counter lives inside #pjax. Increment it, navigate to Home, come back: state is reset because the morph replaced its container with a fresh instance.

Same-page refresh: matched by id

When fez is loaded, Pjax.morphInto uses Fez.nodeMorph, which diffs by id. A form input with a stable id keeps its typed value across same-page refreshes:
